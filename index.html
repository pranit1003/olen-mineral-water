Our Story

Shiv Parvati Foods and Beverage is a leading manufacturer of premium packaged drinking water under the brand name OLEN. We are committed to providing safe, pure, and healthy drinking water to our customers across the region.

With state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and stringent quality control measures, we ensure that every drop of OLEN water meets the highest standards of purity and safety. Our advanced purification technology removes all impurities while retaining essential minerals.

We serve homes, offices, hotels, restaurants, events, and retail outlets, ensuring that pure water is accessible to everyone, everywhere.

To deliver safe, pure, and healthy drinking water to every household and business, making quality hydration accessible to all.

Quality & Purification Process

Advanced multi-stage purification ensuring 99.99% purity

1

Source Water Collection

Water sourced from approved and tested sources, ensuring quality from the very beginning

2

Multi-Stage Filtration

Pre-filtration removes suspended particles, sediments, and large impurities

3

RO Purification

Reverse Osmosis removes dissolved solids, heavy metals, and microscopic contaminants

4

UV Treatment

Ultraviolet sterilization eliminates 99.99% of bacteria, viruses, and microorganisms

5

Ozonation

Ozone treatment provides final disinfection and extends shelf life naturally

6

Mineral Addition

Essential minerals added in balanced quantities for taste and health benefits

7

Quality Testing

Rigorous laboratory testing for pH, TDS, minerals, and microbial parameters

8

Hygienic Packaging

Automated bottling in sterile environment with tamper-proof sealing
